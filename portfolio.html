Concept 01 · Travel & Tour Agency

Travel & Tour Agency In-House Concept HTML + Tailwind
  • A destination, not a stock photo. The hero is Torres del Paine under a real night sky, coordinates and all — because generic beach-and-suitcase imagery is the fastest way for a travel site to look interchangeable.
  • Search built into the hero. Where-to, when, and travel-style fields sit above the fold, so browsing starts in the first five seconds instead of after a scroll.
  • WhatsApp designed in, not bolted on. The chat button is part of the layout from the first mockup — matching how Velmure Tech's actual client sites turn visitors into conversations.

Concept 02 · Car Dealership

Car Dealership In-House Concept HTML + Tailwind
  • A showroom palette, not template blue. Deep navy with a warm gold accent — closer to chrome under showroom lighting than the blue-gradient every auto template reaches for by default.
  • One car, not a stock lot. The hero is a close, specific vehicle — grille, badge, headlight — because a single real car sells harder than a wide shot of an anonymous parking lot.
  • Financing treated as a first-class action. "Finance a Car" sits right next to "Browse Inventory" in the hero — because local buyers decide on monthly affordability as much as the car itself.
